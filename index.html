Hello, I'm Alvar

Earth & Environmental Sciences Student

I am a second-year bachelor's student at University College Utrecht, focusing on geology, climatology, and environmental sciences. I'm interested in the connection between physical Earth processes, environmental sciences, and the geospatial technologies used to analyze the world around us. What motivates me in particular is remote sensing and working to represent complex data simply and beautifully.

This website is a portfolio of my mapping works. Below, you can find my personal mapping projects, as well as the webpage I made for the UU GIS course in 2026. You can also access everything by clicking on "Portfolio Works" in the top right corner. Happy viewing!
